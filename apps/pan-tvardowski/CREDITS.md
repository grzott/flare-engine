# Pan Tvardowski — Credits & Sources

Due-diligence record of every folklore and historical source consulted. Nothing in this game is derived from copyrighted adaptations; all material traces to public-domain legend, 19th-century literature, or Wikipedia summaries of folk tradition.

## Legend & literature (public domain)

- **Pan Twardowski** — Polish folk legend, 16th c. onward. [Wikipedia (EN)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_Twardowski), [Wikipedia (PL)](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_Twardowski).
- Adam Mickiewicz, _Pani Twardowska_ (ballad, 1822) — public domain.
- Józef Ignacy Kraszewski, _Mistrz Twardowski_ (1840) — public domain.
- Historical frame: King Sigismund II Augustus (Zygmunt August), Queen Barbara Radziwiłłówna, Wawel Castle — public-domain historical figures and places.

## Folk bestiary (public domain, Slavic mythology)

All enemy, mini-boss, and boss names derive from Polish / broader Slavic folklore:

- Boruta, Rokita, Licho, Strzyga, Topielec, Mamuna, Nocnica, Południca, Wąpierz, Dziwożona, Leszy (Borowy), Baba Jaga, Bazyliszek Warszawski, Smok Wawelski / Krzemionkowski, Wodnik, Skrzat, Latawiec, Czart, Bies, Mefistofeles, Madej (Zbój Madej).

References: [Polish mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavic_paganism), [Demons in Slavic folklore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slavic_deities_and_creatures). Broadly attested across 19th c. ethnographic works (Kolberg, Gloger) — all public domain.

## Items & artifacts (public domain)

- **Zwierciadło Twardowskiego** (Twardowski's Mirror) — the actual object hangs in the sacristy of the church in Węgrów, Poland.
- **Karczma "Rzym"** (The "Rome" Inn) — canonical legend location.
- **Kogut** (rooster as mount) — canonical in the legend.
- **Pentagram / alchemical sigils** — public-domain occult iconography.

## What this game does NOT use

- **Raptor: Call of the Shadows** (Cygnus Interactive / Apogee) — _no_ art, ship designs, HUD elements, music, level names, or sprites. Only the vertical-scrolling-shmup genre (not copyrightable) influences gameplay.
- **Allegro's "Twardowsky" / "Twardowsky 2.0" CGI shorts** (2015, 2016) — _no_ character design, plot beats, costume, or "time-displaced astronaut" framing. The game's spelling _Tvardowski_ (with a "v") explicitly disambiguates from Allegro's "Twardowsky" (with a "w" + "y").
- Any post-1950 film, comic, or novel adaptation of the legend.
